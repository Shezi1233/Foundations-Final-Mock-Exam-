A falconry centre’s manager asks Claude to “improve the visitor experience” and receives a polished list of ideas. None fits. She had never decided what success looks like, such as fewer queues or more repeat bookings, or what the work would involve.

**Which part of delegation was missing?**

A. Platform awareness: she did not know what Claude can and cannot do.
B. Task delegation: she did not split the work between herself and Claude fairly.
C. Problem awareness: she did not define her goal and what success looks like.
D. Discernment: she should have checked the ideas against a reliable outside source.

## Explanation

Problem awareness means knowing the goal, the work and what success looks like before you type. It is the first part of Delegation, not Discernment.
