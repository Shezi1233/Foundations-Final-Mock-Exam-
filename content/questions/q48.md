An embroidery studio’s associate fills in the Criterion column for the step “Approve each custom-design quote”. The step is reversible, small in value and easy to review, but the studio owner answers for every quote and nothing reviews her decision. The associate writes “reversible, low stakes, Claude has been right in tests”. An auditor will read that column.

**What should the entry say?**

A. Reversible and low stakes, since two of the three criteria agree on AI ownership.
B. Past accuracy, since tests are the best evidence that the step is safe.
C. All three criteria, listed together, since a longer entry is easier to defend.
D. Accountability, the deciding criterion: the step is the owner’s own call.

## Explanation

The criteria are not added up. Name the one carrying the row; where they disagree the strictest wins. This step is the decision somebody answers for, and past accuracy is not a criterion.
