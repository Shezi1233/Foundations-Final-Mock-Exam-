A dairy cooperative’s manager tells Claude: “Keep my inbox manageable. Reply to routine messages, flag important ones, and ask me before doing anything you are unsure about.” Claude must decide which messages are routine and when to ask.

**Which mode of working is this, and where does it most often fail?**

A. Automation, because Claude performs a defined task; the failure is a single step done badly.
B. Augmentation, because they work as thinking partners; the failure is agreeableness.
C. Agency, because Claude chooses many steps toward a goal; the common failure is a misunderstood goal or boundary.
D. Automation, because the manager gave several instructions in one message.

## Explanation

Agency means working toward a goal you set, choosing many steps, often when you are not watching. Its typical failure is a misunderstood goal or boundary, not a badly done single step.
