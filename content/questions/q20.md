An observatory’s associate asks Claude, in a plain chat with no search, about a telescope announcement made last month. Claude answers with confident details. A colleague says Claude has simply “not read the news yet”.

**Which explanation is most accurate?**

A. Claude reads new articles daily but mixed up the dates in this case.
B. Claude’s weights update overnight, so last month’s news should already be known.
C. Claude knows the announcement but hides it unless the question is phrased exactly.
D. Training ended at a cutoff; later events are not in its weights.

## Explanation

Weights freeze after training. Anything after the knowledge cutoff is not in the model; only search or supplied text can bring it in.
