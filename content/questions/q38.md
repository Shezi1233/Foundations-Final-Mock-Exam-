A scooter-rental firm’s associate fills in her Delegation Record for one job. Two lines are empty: the second claim that mattered, and anything Claude marked unverified. She wants to leave them blank and move on.

**Which TWO statements are correct? (Select TWO)**

A. An empty line is a finding: a loop step has not happened yet.
B. She should open a source for a second claim and record what she found.
C. Blank lines are fine, since the record matters less than the final result.
D. She should write none in both, since a full page looks more convincing here.

## Explanation

If a line is empty, a step of the loop has not happened yet for this job. Record what you actually checked; do not fill the page to look finished.
