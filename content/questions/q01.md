A hat maker’s associate lists three worries about a planned workflow. First, the sales spreadsheet names customers. Second, the new tool can send emails and delete files. Third, the tool will rank wholesale applicants and drop the lowest. She wants to sort them under the Case, the Data, the Capability and the People.

**Which pairing of worries to questions is correct?**

A. Names: Capability; send and delete: Data; ranking: Case.
B. Names: People; send and delete: Case; ranking: Capability.
C. Names: Data; send and delete: Capability; ranking: People.
D. Names: Case; send and delete: People; ranking: Data.

## Explanation

Customer names are information entering the tool (Data). Send and delete are authority being added (Capability). Ranking applicants and dropping the lowest affects people who may be excluded unfairly (People).
