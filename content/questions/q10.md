A circus’s associate asks Claude for the touring schedule and ticket prices of a small village circus that has no website and was mentioned once in a local paper. Claude replies with a tidy table of dates, venues and prices. A friend who knows the circus says all of it is wrong.

**Which explanation is most accurate?**

A. Claude malfunctioned, and a software update would remove errors of this kind.
B. Claude lied on purpose, since it knew the real schedule and hid it.
C. Little text existed, so it continued plausibly, and nothing inside it checks truth.
D. Claude searched an outdated page and copied the old schedule into the table.

## Explanation

The model predicts a likely continuation. With thin source text it produces plausible invention, and there is no internal truth-checker. This is the machine working as built, not a malfunction or a lie.
