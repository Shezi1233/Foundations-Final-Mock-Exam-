An ink manufacturer’s associate asks Claude for current prices from three pigment suppliers and adds a line: for each price, say whether you found it by searching just now or already knew it, and give the page date. Claude lists three prices, each marked “searched”, with links, but no dates.

**What should she check first?**

A. Nothing more, since each price is marked searched and has a link.
B. Open one link, find the supporting sentence, and find the page date.
C. Ask Claude again whether it really searched, and accept its answer as evidence.
D. Add today’s date beside each price so that the whole list looks more current.

## Explanation

A citation is not verification; checking the citation is. A source is also not a date, so find the page date yourself.
