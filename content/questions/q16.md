A lock maker’s associate sees an error in the order system and types to Claude, “Something about stock is wrong.” Claude’s reply is generic. The full error message is still on her screen, and she wants a better answer.

**Which TWO habits help most? (Select TWO)**

A. Show the screenshot, since showing beats describing it from memory.
B. Ask what a capable colleague would need to see, then attach it.
C. Send a longer description of how frustrating the error is for the team.
D. Ask Claude to guess the likely cause from the single word stock.

## Explanation

Context is the whole game: put the real material in front of the model. Emotion and guessing from one word add nothing.
