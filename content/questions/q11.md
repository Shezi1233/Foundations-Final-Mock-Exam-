A rug-weaving cooperative’s associate proposes that Claude make the final decision on which of 200 member households receive a hardship grant, with no one reviewing individual cases. The grant decides people’s basic income, and the cooperative’s board answers for each award. No review of Claude’s output could repair a wrongly refused household.

**How should the case be classified?**

A. Fully appropriate, since Claude can apply the written criteria consistently.
B. Inappropriate: the consequence and the responsibility cannot be repaired by any review.
C. Appropriate with review, since the board can sign the list at the end.
D. Appropriate with review, since sampling a few cases would catch most errors.

## Explanation

High consequence, hard to undo, and accountability cannot transfer to a tool. A gate cannot repair this, so the final decision stays human-owned.
