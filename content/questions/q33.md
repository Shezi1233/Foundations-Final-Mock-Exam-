A seed bank’s associate repeats the same cleanup every week on files that live in 40 folders on her own computer, and she is tired of uploading them to a browser chat each time. She wants the scripts saved and wants them to fix their own errors.

**Which surface is most appropriate?**

A. Stay in the browser chat, since it is the zero-risk place to learn.
B. Claude Code or OpenCode, which sees her folders and keeps the scripts.
C. A custom GPT, since it keeps scripts portable across every tool.
D. A fresh chat each week, with the files uploaded in batches of ten.

## Explanation

Uploading becoming the annoying part is the graduation signal. A terminal agent sees your folders, keeps scripts and heals its own errors.
