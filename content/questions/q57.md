A property firm’s associate asks Claude for a weekly rent-arrears summary. The first answer already ignores the required format and the audience. Her instinct is to switch to the most capable model before changing anything else.

**Which step does the diagnostic sequence place first?**

A. Switch to the flagship model, since depth fixes most disappointing output.
B. Re-read the prompt against its five parts: role, context, task, constraints and format.
C. Conclude that the task is an expectation mismatch and drop it.
D. Audit the Project’s standing instructions and knowledge files for staleness.

## Explanation

Wrong from the first answer points to under-specification, and the sequence runs cheapest first: prompt, session length, feature and model, configuration, then the task itself.
