A bakery’s associate keeps one chat open all day: a workout plan, then a spreadsheet bug, then a thank-you note to her aunt. Answers have become vaguer, and Claude has just broken a formatting limit she set five turns ago.

**What is the most appropriate step?**

A. Send one more clarifying prompt repeating all of her earlier instructions.
B. Save anything worth keeping, then start a new chat and paste in only the facts that matter.
C. Switch to thinking mode, since vague answers mean the task is too hard.
D. Ask Claude to apologise, since that resets the context window.

## Explanation

This is context rot: a long, mixed chat dilutes the signal and older turns get compacted. Reset beats rescue: start fresh with only the facts that matter, after saving anything valuable.
