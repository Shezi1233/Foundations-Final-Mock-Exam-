A dye works’ workflow for supplier-email summaries was approved six months ago. Since then the vendor added a feature that reads incoming supplier emails, the model was upgraded, and a few small, unrelated things changed around the office.

**Which TWO changes should trigger a re-check of the relevant block? (Select TWO)**

A. The vendor added a feature that reads incoming supplier emails.
B. The model was upgraded to a newer version since the last decision.
C. The workflow’s name was shortened in the team’s shared calendar.
D. The associate’s desk moved to a different floor of the building last month.

## Explanation

Re-check when the model, feature, connector, data, audience, permission, policy or consequence changes. Renaming a calendar entry or moving a desk changes nothing that was approved.
