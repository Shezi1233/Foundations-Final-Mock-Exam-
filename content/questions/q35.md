A calligraphy school’s teacher needs one quick, one-time explanation of how to compare two inks for tomorrow’s class. A colleague suggests building a Skill and connecting Drive.

**What does the task need?**

A. A Skill, since it can capture the comparison method for later.
B. A Connector, since the notes may exist in Drive somewhere.
C. Neither; a good prompt is enough for a one-off request.
D. Both, since a careful teacher should prepare for repeat requests.

## Explanation

A task done once is just a good prompt. A Skill is worth making only when the task repeats; a connector you do not need is an open door.
