A lantern-festival organiser’s associate wants Claude to reconcile 80 vendor invoices, which carry vendor names, bank account numbers and amounts. The task needs the account numbers, so redaction is not possible. Nobody has confirmed whether her Claude workspace is approved for this kind of data.

**Which TWO statements are correct? (Select TWO)**

A. Because the specifics are the substance, redaction is unavailable here.
B. The prior question is whether this route is approved for this data.
C. A sandbox or Project setting would settle the approval question for her.
D. She can decide at the keyboard under deadline, since the data stays internal.

## Explanation

If the task needs the identifiers, redaction is off the table and the question becomes whether this route is approved for this data. A sandbox or Project is a route or boundary, not an approval.
