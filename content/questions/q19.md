A carousel maker’s associate trains a new colleague once on the firm’s quoting rules and then expects Claude to remember them in the same way. Each new chat quotes without the rules, and she re-explains them every time.

**What is the most appropriate fix?**

A. Tell Claude more firmly in each chat that it must remember the rules.
B. Put the rules where Claude reads them automatically, such as standing instructions.
C. Wait a few weeks, since Claude learns the rules gradually from use.
D. Explain the rules once to a different assistant that remembers people.

## Explanation

Claude is stateless; it starts every chat from the same frozen weights. Stable rules belong in standing instructions (or a Project) so they load automatically.
