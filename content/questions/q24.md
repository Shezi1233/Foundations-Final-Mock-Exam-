A cotton-ginning mill’s associate asks Cowork for a plan before it summarises supplier quotes. The plan shows it will also open last year’s contracts folder, which she never mentioned, and will email the summary to all suppliers.

**Which TWO plan checks does this fail? (Select TWO)**

A. Scope: it touches data and folders that she did not name.
B. Reach: it proposes sending email that she never intended to send.
C. Order: it verifies the evidence before it changes anything at all.
D. Assumptions: it states the audience and the format plainly, not silently.

## Explanation

The four plan checks are scope, order, reach and assumptions. An unnamed folder fails scope; an unintended email fails reach. The last two options describe passing behaviour.
