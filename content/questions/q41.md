An amusement park’s associate is writing the “If it goes wrong” section of the delegation map for Claude-drafted ride-closure notices. Last month, wrong reopening times went out for six days before anyone paused the workflow.

**Which entry is most appropriate?**

A. “Stopped by the duty manager without permission; scope checked by counting notices.”
B. “Stopped by the CEO after a board meeting; scope assumed to be small.”
C. “Stopped when a complaint arrives; map left as it is to avoid confusion.”
D. “Stopped by the associate once Claude itself says that the draft was wrong.”

## Explanation

Decide in advance who can stop it without permission and how you will count how many outputs were affected. Do not assume scope is small or wait for the model to confess.
