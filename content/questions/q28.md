A salt-works associate approved Claude’s plan to compare three packaging quotes. When the run ends, she puts the approved plan beside what Claude did and notices that it also visited a fourth supplier’s website, which the plan never listed.

**What does she do with the difference?**

A. Ignore it, since the final table looks complete and entirely correct to her.
B. Delete the fourth supplier’s data and say nothing more about it.
C. Treat each difference as a question for Claude, then verify the answer.
D. Rewrite the whole plan next time so that Claude can never deviate.

## Explanation

An approved plan is not a cage, but a silent change is the failure. Every difference between plan and run is a question to ask, then verify.
