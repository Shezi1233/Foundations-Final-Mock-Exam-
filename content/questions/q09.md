A stained-glass restorer’s team uses AI every day. They reach their goals, keep client details private and are honest about AI’s role, but each task is re-run ten times, burning hours and tokens before anyone is satisfied.

**Which quality of AI fluency is weakest?**

A. Effective, because they are not reaching their goals at all.
B. Ethical, because they are not honest about AI’s role in the work.
C. Safe, because they are exposing clients’ privacy and security.
D. Efficient, because they waste time, tokens and energy on re-runs.

## Explanation

The four qualities are effective, efficient, ethical and safe. Goals are met (effective), honesty is intact (ethical), privacy is kept (safe). The waste of re-runs is an efficiency failure.
