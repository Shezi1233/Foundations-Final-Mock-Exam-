A fountain-pen maker’s associate gives Claude a six-line brief to compare three nib suppliers. The Authority line says nothing about unlisted suppliers. Claude silently drops one supplier and adds another, and the final table looks fine.

**Which Authority line would have prevented the silent change?**

A. “Choose whichever suppliers seem best; the final table will show what you used.”
B. “You may swap a supplier if needed, but tell me when you did.”
C. “Ask me before every single step of the comparison, however small it is.”
D. “Use any suppliers you like and keep the method entirely to yourself afterwards.”

## Explanation

“Choose, but say when you chose”: flexibility is allowed, silent decisions are not. A decision you cannot see is one you cannot review.
