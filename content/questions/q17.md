A sock factory’s owner asks Claude to critique his plan to sell socks through subscription boxes. Claude replies with warm praise and no weaknesses. He wants a critique that forces Claude to look properly.

**Which request is most appropriate?**

A. “Score the plan 1 to 10 on market, margins and risk, justifying each.”
B. “Tell me honestly whether the plan is good and how sure you are.”
C. “Find the strongest evidence that the plan will succeed in the first year.”
D. “Rewrite the plan so that it sounds more convincing to investors and banks.”

## Explanation

Named criteria with a score force the model to look and counter sycophancy. “Find evidence it will succeed” and “make it convincing” hand it the conclusion; “be honest” has no criteria.
