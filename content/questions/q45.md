A tent-hire company’s associate on a Team plan notices that Claude never remembers her preferences across chats and assumes memory is broken. Her friend on a personal plan sees Claude remembering preferences.

**What is the most likely explanation?**

A. On Team plans, memory is off by default until an owner enables it.
B. Memory is broken, and starting a fresh chat each day will fix it.
C. Team plans never allow memory at all, whatever settings the owner chooses to use.
D. Claude forgets because the model’s weights are reset every single night by default.

## Explanation

Claude turns memory on by default for individual plans and leaves it off on Team and Enterprise until an owner enables it. Confirm the plan and who holds the setting.
