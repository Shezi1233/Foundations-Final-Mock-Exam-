A clock repairer’s associate wants Claude to include the current retail price of a replacement movement from three named suppliers in her quote plan. The suppliers’ prices change weekly, and the rest of the plan is a synthesis task.

**How should the price step be handled?**

A. Let Claude fill the prices from what it already knows, then check the totals.
B. Ask for each price with a source she can open and its date.
C. Move the price step to the owner, since AI cannot handle prices at all.
D. Ask for a range from memory, then use the midpoint in the quote plan.

## Explanation

Window test: current prices are outside what the model knows. For anything current, ask for a source you can open, with its date, instead of a confident answer.
