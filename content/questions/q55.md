A colleague hands an associate a Skill built by another team inside her own company. She plans to enable it because “internal means vetted”, and she reasons that she can limit its reach by starting it read-only, as she does with connectors.

**What is the most appropriate step?**

A. Enable it, since a Skill from inside the company is already vetted.
B. Enable it and narrow its scope, since a Skill has a scope setting like a connector.
C. Open the package and confirm with its publisher what it accesses and why, and that its reach still matches current policy.
D. Decline it, since any Skill that did not come from Anthropic is unsafe.

## Explanation

Internal does not mean vetted. A Skill has no scope dial: it runs with whatever access its chat has, so the control is reading it first. Scope is a connector idea.
