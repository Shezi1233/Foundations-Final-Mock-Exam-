A podcast network’s associate asks Claude for a finished 600-word blog post arguing that short episodes keep listeners. The draft is fluent but argues the weaker point first, and each fix means rewriting whole paragraphs. She has one day.

**Which approach is most appropriate next time?**

A. Ask for the draft again, using a longer prompt with more style rules.
B. Ask for arguments and counter-arguments, then outlines, and only then the draft.
C. Ask for three finished drafts and pick the best to polish.
D. Ask Claude to critique its own draft and rewrite it in one go.

## Explanation

Brainstorm-iterate loop: research first, then options and outlines, then the draft. Structure decisions are cheap at the outline level and expensive at the draft level.
