A pearl farm’s associate describes her AI-assisted export-paperwork workflow to the compliance lead. Claude fills each export form from the shipment record, and a named officer approves every form before it is filed. Claude can miss an obligation the shipment record implies but never states. The lead will decide whether to keep the workflow.

**Which description is most appropriate?**

A. “Claude fills the forms; an officer approves each one. Watch for implied obligations.”
B. “Our AI handles export paperwork, with an officer available for any questions that arise.”
C. “Export paperwork is now fully automated and as accurate as our officers.”
D. “Forms now take minutes instead of hours; the officer’s role is minimal.”

## Explanation

State what the tool does, then name the human checkpoint, and say its known limit. The other options overstate (“fully automated”, “as good as a person”) or hide the gate (“AI handles”).
