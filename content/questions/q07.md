A cable-car operator’s associate asks Claude for evidence that ski-lift passes sell better in bundles. Claude replies, “Bundled passes lifted sales by 63.4 percent across European resorts in 2025,” with no source. She plans to put the figure in a board paper tomorrow.

**What is the most appropriate step?**

A. Use the figure, since a number that specific is likely to be from real data.
B. Ask Claude how confident it is and use the figure if it says high.
C. Ask for a source she can open, and check it before using the figure.
D. Round the figure to 60 percent so that it looks less precise.

## Explanation

An over-exact figure with no source is a classic sign of invention. Verification means opening the source, finding the supporting sentence and checking the date. Claude’s own confidence is not evidence.
