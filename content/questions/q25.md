A snow-plough contractor’s associate each month fetches the invoice list from Drive and formats the client summary in the firm’s exact layout, with a withholding flag. She is deciding whether she needs a Skill, a Connector, both or neither.

**What does she need?**

A. Both: a Connector for the Drive data, a Skill for the layout.
B. A Skill only, since the layout is the only repeated part of the work.
C. A Connector only, since the data is what lives in another app each month.
D. Neither, since a well-written prompt covers any monthly task she repeats.

## Explanation

Re-fetching data from another app signals a Connector; re-explaining how to format signals a Skill. Both frictions are present.
