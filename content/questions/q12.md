A heritage railway’s associate redesigns the process for its monthly customer newsletter, drafted by Claude and sent by the duty editor. Readers complain more about uneven layouts and tone than about delays. Before changing the process she must choose what to measure.

**Which metric should she use, and when should she measure it?**

A. Time per newsletter, measured after the change, from memory of the old process.
B. Revision rounds only, measured after the change from the first few new issues.
C. Accuracy of every figure, measured before the change by asking Claude to estimate.
D. Consistency of layout and tone, measured on one unchanged run before changing anything.

## Explanation

Pick the metric that matches why the workflow matters (here, consistency). Measure the baseline once, before the change, on an unchanged run. You cannot rebuild a baseline afterwards.
