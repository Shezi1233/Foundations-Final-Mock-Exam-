A lighthouse trust’s associate asks Claude whether a planned coastal-path extension is legally permitted. Claude answers with a flat “Yes” in four assured sentences. A planning specialist would say “it depends” and ask for the parish boundary and the date of the designation.

**Which sign of a made-up answer does this show?**

A. Specifics that are too exact, such as a figure quoted to two decimals.
B. Confidence where an expert would hesitate and say it depends.
C. Contradiction across a long output, between its start and its end.
D. A claimed action that never happened, such as a sent email.

## Explanation

One of four discernment signs: confidence where an expert would hesitate. Ask what would change the answer.
