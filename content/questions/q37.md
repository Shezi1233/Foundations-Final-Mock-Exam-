An audiobook studio’s producer drops a 60-minute meeting recording into Claude and asks for decisions, open questions and action items by owner. She plans to quote two team members in an email to the director.

**Which TWO habits are most appropriate? (Select TWO)**

A. Ask for decisions, open questions and action items grouped by owner.
B. Check who said what before quoting anyone in the email.
C. Quote the summary’s wording directly, since it came from the recording.
D. Assume the action items are right, since audio is easy to transcribe.

## Explanation

Meeting recordings are valuable context. Speaker attribution is weaker than transcription, so check who said what before quoting.
