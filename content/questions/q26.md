A luggage maker’s operations lead wants one package for the sales team that bundles a quote-writing method, a connection to the CRM and a few shortcut commands. She is unsure whether she needs a Skill, a connector or something else.

**Which describes what she needs?**

A. A Project, since it stores the context for one stream of work.
B. A Skill, since it stores the method and also holds the CRM link.
C. A custom GPT, since it carries portable skills into every other tool.
D. A plugin, since it bundles skills, outside connections and sometimes commands.

## Explanation

A plugin packages skills with connections to outside apps and sometimes commands, aimed at a whole kind of work.
