A glassblowing studio’s associate runs a Cowork task that drafts a reply to each customer enquiry and can also send it. She is considering Auto mode so that it never interrupts her. The studio’s customers include large corporate buyers, and one reply can commit the studio to a price.

**Which setting is most appropriate for this workflow?**

A. Skip, since drafting is cheap and she reads replies later anyway.
B. Manual, since sending is a high-consequence action that needs her approval.
C. Auto, since interruptions are costly and Claude screens every action.
D. Auto for the first week, then Skip once the drafts look good.

## Explanation

Sending as you, with a price commitment, is the highest rung of the autonomy ladder. Use Manual. Auto or Skip suit proven, bounded, low-consequence work.
