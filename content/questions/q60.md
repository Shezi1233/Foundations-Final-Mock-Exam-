A seed merchant’s associate delegates a multi-source market scan that will run for several minutes while she is in a meeting. She will not be able to watch it, and a wrong direction would waste the whole run.

**What is the cheapest moment to catch a wrong direction, and how does she reach it?**

A. After the run, by reading the finished table line by line.
B. Before the run: ask for the plan first, read it, change one step, then say go.
C. During the run, by writing every step as a recipe so Claude cannot deviate.
D. After the run, by asking Claude whether it is confident in the result.

## Explanation

Plan-first is the cheapest moment in any job: a plan costs a minute to read and a sentence to fix, while the same mistake found after the run costs the run.
