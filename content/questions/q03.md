A foundry’s associate asks Claude to total quarterly scrap by month. Claude’s script groups by calendar month, but the foundry’s fiscal year starts in July, and “Pending” means scrap not yet verified. The totals look plausible and are wrong. The associate knew both facts and never stated them.

**Which section of the five-part brief was missing?**

A. Goal: what problem is solved when the script works, in her own words.
B. Input: what files she is giving, and their rough size and format.
C. Output: exactly what she wants back, such as a table or a total.
D. Rules: the constraints a stranger would not know, such as the fiscal year.

## Explanation

Rules hold the limits a stranger would not know: the fiscal-year start and what “Pending” means. Every wrong analysis traces back to a rule you knew and did not state.
