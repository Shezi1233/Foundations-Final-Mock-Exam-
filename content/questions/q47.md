A stamp-collecting society’s associate drafts a members’ report with Claude. The society’s funding contract requires members to be told when AI drafted a report. She thinks it does not matter, since Claude only reformatted a few tables.

**Which TWO statements are correct? (Select TWO)**

A. Disclosure rules come first: if a contract requires it, the decision is already made.
B. Only if nothing requires it should she ask whether AI would change readers’ understanding.
C. She can skip disclosure, since her own view of the work decides the matter.
D. She should disclose only if a member asks after reading the report.

## Explanation

Ask in order: is disclosure required by law, policy or contract? If a rule answers it, you are done. Only then use judgment about readers’ understanding.
